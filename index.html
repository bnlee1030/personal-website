About me

Hello, I’m Brendan Lee.

I grew up in New Jersey, studied at Stevens Institute of Technology, and now live in New York City.

I’ve studied classical piano since I was a child. At the moment I’m working on Scriabin’s Piano Sonata No. 2 and Liszt’s Rhapsodie espagnole.

I’m an avid skier, and I love hiking and getting outdoors whenever I can.

I want to spend my life on things that are fun and fulfilling, and keep learning as much as I can along the way. This site is where I’ll share some of that.
